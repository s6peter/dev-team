# Political Catalyst Tracker

This log tracks public policy, regulatory, legislative, contract, court, and agency catalysts that may affect sectors, companies, or ETFs.

## Rules

- Use public sources only.
- Do not use hacked, leaked, private, or non-public information.
- Do not track private personal lives.
- Do not create signals based on gossip, identity, friendship, or personal attacks.
- Track sector/company impact, confidence, and false-signal risk.
- Political catalyst tracking cannot approve trades.

## Review Checklist

- Is the source public?
- Is the catalyst direct or indirect?
- Which sector or ticker is affected?
- Is the effect likely already priced in?
- What could make this signal false?
- Should this be ignored, monitored, researched more, or sent to Market Research?
