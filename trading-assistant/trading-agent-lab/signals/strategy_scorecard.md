# Strategy Scorecard

Use this scorecard before sending any paper-trade candidate to the Risk Manager.

## Scoring

| Category | Weight | Score 0-5 | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Catalyst quality | 20 | 0 | Is there a clear public catalyst? |
| Evidence quality | 20 | 0 | Are sources fresh, public, and reliable? |
| Liquidity / tradability | 15 | 0 | Is the asset liquid and allowed? |
| Risk/reward | 20 | 0 | Is upside/downside defined and reasonable? |
| Invalidation clarity | 15 | 0 | Is the "we are wrong" condition clear? |
| Policy risk | 10 | 0 | Does policy or regulation create unusual risk? |

## Decision Bands

- 80-100: Eligible for Risk Manager paper-trade review.
- 60-79: Watchlist or needs more information.
- 0-59: Reject.
